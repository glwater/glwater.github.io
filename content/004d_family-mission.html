    Our Mission
    ❝As citizens of the Grand Lakes community in Katy, Fort Bend County, Texas, we are adamant about preventing the four Water Districts (MUD 4, MUD 1, MUD 2, and WCID) serving our community from prioritizing private profit over public welfare. Through community monitoring, we advocate for, and demand, Public Interest Safeguards: Absolute Integrity from our elected officials and all consultants serving our community, Impeccable Representation, Lawful Governance, Responsible Spending, Transparent Contracting, Vendor Accountability, and Rigorous State Oversight.❞

    🚨 Grand Lakes Civic Awareness & Family Education Guide
    📋 Part 4: The Family Neighborhood Mission Checklist


    🕵️‍♂️ Family Mission Checklist: My Local Government Investigation

    Hey Junior Detectives! Grab a clipboard and a pencil. It's time to investigate how our local neighborhood works. Use this checklist to complete your mission and help keep Grand Lakes a great place to live!

    📋 The Family Neighborhood Mission Checklist

    Print this out and keep it on the kitchen counter as you complete your crossword puzzle research!

    🟩 Mission 1: The Crossword Warm-Up ~ The Codebreaker

    Before you head to the computer or walk the neighborhood, test your skills on the puzzle.

    • Decode the Clues: Read through the crossword puzzle with an adult.
    • Fill the Blanks: Use the Word Bank to find words like Transparency, Fiduciary Duty, and Accountability.
    • Find 3 words in the puzzle that talk about money or taxes.
    • Find 2 words that describe what a good community leader should have (Hint: look for Integrity and Accountability).
    • Define the Big Words: Pick 2 words you've never heard before and ask an adult to help you define them.
    • Look up what a MUD (Municipal Utility District) stands for and explain it to a family member.

    🟦 Mission 2: The Digital Scout ~ Online Super-Sleuth

    With the help of a parent, open up a web browser and visit your local district website or search online.

    • Look up what a WCID (Water Control & Improvement District) stands for and explain it to a family member.
    • Know Your MUD/WCID: Do you live in MUD 1, MUD 2, MUD 4, or the WCID? Write it down here: ________________________.
    • Locate the district's website: Locate the website for the district that serves your household. Write the URL down here: ________________________.
    • Find out the name of at least one Board Director for your local Grand Lakes water district.
    • Locate the Budget: Find the most recent budget summary on the district's website.
    • Locate the Financial Report: Find the most recent financial report on the district's website.
    • Track the Income: Where does the district get its money? (Hint: Look for "Tax Rate" or "Service Fees").
    • The "Follow the Money" Challenge: Find at least one fee paid to a private consultant firm (like an attorney, bookkeeper, auditor, engineer, or tax collector).
    • Locate the official "Open Records" or "Public Information Act" contact details on the district website.
    • Visit glwater.github.io with a parent and read the latest update.

    🟥 Mission 3: The Deep Dive ~ Take It to School & Community

    Civics is teamwork! Share what you've learned with friends and teachers.

    • The Teacher Chat: Show the crossword puzzle to your Social Studies, Civics, or History teacher. Ask if your class can do it together!
    • The Meeting Mission: Check the district's website to find the date of the next local Board meeting. Mark it on the family calendar.
    • Kitchen Table Debate: Sit down with your family at dinner and discuss: What is the difference between an honest community servant and someone just working for private interests?
    • Having looked up the district's annual budget, is it balanced, or are they overspending?
    • Find out when the next local board election takes place. Write the date down here: ________________________.
    • Work with your family to write a quick email sharing what you discovered during this challenge!

    🏆 Mission Accomplished!

    Once you have checked off all the boxes, you are officially a Grand Lakes Community Protector. Share your findings, stories, or what you learned with your neighbors to keep the chain of awareness going!
