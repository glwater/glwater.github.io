    Our Mission
    ❝As citizens of the Grand Lakes community in Katy, Fort Bend County, Texas, we are adamant about preventing the four Water Districts (MUD 4, MUD 1, MUD 2, and WCID) serving our community from prioritizing private profit over public welfare. Through community monitoring, we advocate for, and demand, Public Interest Safeguards: Absolute Integrity from our elected officials and all consultants serving our community, Impeccable Representation, Lawful Governance, Responsible Spending, Transparent Contracting, Vendor Accountability, and Rigorous State Oversight.❞

    The Principles of Lawful Governance


    A governance framework for Municipal Utility Districts and Water Control and Improvement Districts must be built on four cross-cutting commitments:

    • Independence: Decisions made free from improper influence and conflicts of interest.
    • Governance: Competent, responsible, effective, and lawful administration.
    • Justice: Fair, impartial, and equitable treatment.
    • Integrity and the Rule of Law: Ethical conduct and consistent adherence to the law.

    To help our community explore the foundations, principles, and values of water district governance (Independence, Transparency, Compliance, Oversight, Justice, and Accountability), we present the following acronyms. While trivial and meaningful, this framework is not a substitute for applicable Texas law and the district's statutory duties.

    COAT — A framework for lawful governance: The protective coat of good governance.
    C — Compliance: Adherence to applicable laws, regulations, contracts, policies, and legal duties.
    O — Oversight: Independent scrutiny, effective controls, and appropriate review of decisions and expenditures.
    A — Accountability: Responsibility for decisions, public resources, performance, and the consequences of misconduct.
    T — Transparency: Open decision-making, accessible records, meaningful public participation, and honest communication, subject to lawful confidentiality requirements.

    CLEAN — Clean and ethical government: Ethical administration, freedom from corruption, and fidelity to the law.
    C — Compliance
    L — Law and the Rule of Law
    E — Ethics and Integrity
    A — Accountability
    N — Neutrality or Nonpartisanship.

    TRUST — The public-trust standard: Public trust is the ultimate objective of lawful governance. Stewardship and truthfulness broaden the framework to emphasize responsible management of public resources and honest communication.
    T — Transparency
    R — Rule of Law
    U — Unbiased Justice
    S — Stewardship
    T — Truthfulness.

    LIGHT — Governance in the open: A positive metaphor for openness, ethical leadership, and illuminating public decisions. Oversight and accountability can be supporting requirements within the framework.
    L — Lawfulness
    I — Integrity
    G — Good Governance
    H — Honesty
    T — Transparency.

    RIGHT — Doing government right: Communicates lawful conduct and ethical administration in plain language. Justice, compliance, and accountability could be explicit supporting standards.
    R — Rule of Law
    I — Independence
    G — Good Governance
    H — Honesty
    T — Transparency.

    GOVERNANCE as a ten-part framework
    G — Good Governance
    O — Oversight
    V — Verifiable Decisions and Records
    E — Ethics and Integrity
    R — Rule of Law
    N — Neutrality and Independence
    A — Accountability
    N — Non-arbitrariness and Equal Treatment
    C — Compliance
    E — Equity and Justice.
