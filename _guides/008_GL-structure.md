Grand Lakes is a community in Katy, Fort Bend County, Texas, with 38 commercial entities and 2,739 residences falling within the jurisdiction of a Water Control and Improvement District ([WCID](https://districtdirectory.org/fort-bend-county/grand-lakes-water-control-and-improvement-district/)) and three Municipal Utility Districts (MUDs)–[MUD No. 1](https://www.grandlakesmud1.com/), [MUD No. 2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/), [MUD No. 4](https://www.grandlakesmud4.com/). The water districts are local governments (political subdivisions of the state. Households and businesses within the Grand Lakes community are also members of the Grand Lakes Community Association (GLCA), which has the responsibility for neighborhood upkeep. GLCA is a non-profit corporation registered with Taxpayer ID\# 32000119951 and Texas SOS File Number 0145900101. 

The four Grand Lakes water districts are within Houston’s extraterritorial jurisdiction (ETJ), a five-mile band around Houston’s general-purpose boundaries. Each of the three MUDs has a Strategic Partnership Agreement (SPA) with the City of Houston, through which the 1% sales and use tax paid by buyers to retailers is split equally (0.5% for each) between the City and the MUD.

Covering 1,390 acres (2.17 sq mi; 1.4737 mi x 1.4737 mi; or 7,781 ft x 7,781 ft), the four Grand Lakes water districts serve land, businesses, and households as summarized below. This area is only 0.25% that of Fort Bend County (862.1 sq mi \| [census data](https://data.census.gov/profile/Fort_Bend_County,_Texas?g=050XX00US48157)). Because of the very small area they cover, the four Grand Lakes water districts should be one district. Instead, the four districts are administered as separate entities, but by the same consultants; the Tax Collector being an exception (*see* the article ["Know the Consultants Serving the Grand Lakes Water Districts"](/guides/011_GLWD-consultants/)). This scheme is the perfect recipe for conflict of interest, lack of independent governance, multiplicate fees and charges, and more. The scheme is exploited to the fullest by the different consultants, causing much harm to our community.
