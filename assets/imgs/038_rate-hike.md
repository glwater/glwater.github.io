### Secretive Meetings and Engineered Models: The Truth Behind the Grand Lakes MUD No. 2 Rate Hike
In early 2024, [Grand Lakes Municipal Utility District (MUD) No. 2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/) implemented an unprecedented rate order that has left local households facing massive increases on their monthly water bills. Dated December 18, 2023, and signed on January 29, 2024, the rate order effectively spiked monthly water utility bills for residents by 100% to 350% (representing a new-to-old ratio of 200% to 450%). The structural changes are calculated to extract an additional annual income of at least $409,200 from the community, based on an added burden of roughly $50 per month across the MUD’s 682 households ($50 × 12 × 682). 

What makes this financial burden particularly striking?
- The timing―the rate hike was pushed through at a moment when the MUD carried zero debt, possessed no outstanding bonds, and held over $9 million in cash reserves.

- The secrecy―this rate framework was not the byproduct of open public deliberation, but was instead orchestrated behind closed doors through non-public communications and unposted meetings, in stark violation of violation of Government Code §§ [551.021](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.021), [551.041](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.041), [551.042](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.042), and [551.043](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.043).

### The Unlawful December 6, 2023 Private Meeting
The foundation of the new rate structure was finalized on the morning of December 6, 2023, at 9:30 AM. A meeting was held at the private offices of the district’s water system operator, [Municipal District Services](https://mdswater.com/), located at 406 W. Grand Pkwy S, Suite 260, Katy, Texas. 

In attendance were:
* **Bradley Allen Baker** (MUD Board Member).
* **Michael Christopher McClusky** (MUD Board Member).
* **Taylor Watson** (MUD Bookkeeper, [Municipal Accounts & Consulting](https://municipalaccounts.com/)).
* **[Charlie Chapline](https://mdswater.com/meet-the-team/)** (MUD Water System Operator, [Municipal District Services](https://mdswater.com/)).

**[Christopher Todd Skinner](https://www.sphllp.com/_chris_skinner.html)**, **[Gordon Christopher Cranner](https://www.sphllp.com/_gordon_cranner.html)**, and **Melia Berry**―staff representing the MUD Attorney, [Schwartz, Page & Harding](https://www.sphllp.com/) received email updates on the progress and achievements.

Under the Texas [Open Meetings Act](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=) (Government Code Chapter 551), governing boards are strictly prohibited from deliberating public business, engineering financial models, or setting utility rates in private. For this December 6 meeting, *no public notice was posted, no public access was granted, and no official minutes were recorded*. This constitutes a severe and direct violation of Government Code §§ [551.021](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.021), [551.041](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.041), [551.042](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.042), and [551.043](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.043).

### The Paper Trail: Premeditated Rate Engineering and Bypassing Public Governance
Reviewing the internal email trails reveals that the public vote on December 18 was merely an endorsement of a pre-fabricated framework. 

On **November 28, 2023**, then-board member Michael Christopher McClusky explicitly initiated the off-the-record gathering via email, writing to the district's consultants: 
> *"Brad [Bradley Allen Baker] and I would like to meet with you both regarding our revenue changes going forward... Charlie, We would like to meet at your place [Municipal District Services private offices] if that is an option."*

On **December 5, 2023**—the day before the private meeting—MUD Bookkeeper Taylor Watson of [Municipal Accounts & Consulting](https://municipalaccounts.com/) openly admitted in writing that the district’s financial modeling was being actively manipulated outside public oversight to force an escalation in rates:
> *"The revenues for 2024 do not match the actual 2024 budget, this was purposeful because we knew we would be changing the rate order..."*

On **December 14, 2023**, following the unposted meeting, Operator [Charlie Chapline](https://mdswater.com/meet-the-team/) circulated the final rate forecasting documentation, explicitly confirming via email that the attached files *"captured everything that was discussed"* during the private December 6 session. 

On **December 18, 2023**, a public MUD board meeting convened ([Agenda](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/static/55eb275284eb2d4a0965c6acb4937e7a/12_18_23_Agenda_GL_MUD_2_2eb955c87a.pdf) \| [Minutes](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/static/6f2cd9293360e4a960dc863323b089e7/12_18_2024_Minutes_1dc5ea670f.pdf)). The full rate package was introduced to the public ledger. Board Member Bradley Allen Baker, who had actively participated in the illegal private negotiations and email trails, put forth the formal motion to approve the pre-fabricated rate schedule. 

On **January 29, 2024**, the pre-fabricated rate order was signed. It came out carrying an effective date of February 1, 2023.

On **February 19, 2024**, the pre-fabricated rate order was [announced](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/posts/2024-02-19/rate-order-change-notice) to the residents / ratepayers on the MUD's website.

On **February 26, 2024**, [Schwartz, Page & Harding](https://www.sphllp.com/) paralegal Melia Berry altered the rate order's effective date to **February 1, 2024** and uploaded it to the MUD website, without:
* Any public announcement or notice to residents.
* Distributing a revised rate order to the community.
* Conducting a new public board discussion or vote.
* Re-signing or properly re-executing the altered order.

On **May 20, 2024**, a public MUD board meeting convened ([Agenda](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/static/134a63fb0ae3998daaaa0cf56f20ca3b/May_20_2024_Agenda_9862_6693840b9a.pdf) \| [Minutes](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/static/1470bd9c53af4bf0dfbd030ebe217084/05_20_24_Approved_Minutes_GL_MUD_2_70620faf4c.pdf)). Bradley Allen Baker—who participated in the secretive December 5, 2023 meeting and the secretive communications, and who on December 18, 2023 moved to approve the pre-fabricated rate schedule—moved to engage Austin-based law firm of [Lloyd Gosselink Rochelle Townsend](https://www.lglawfirm.com/) as legal counsel to crush the residents’ protest that challenged the fraudulent rate order to the Public Utility Commission of Texas ([PUCT](https://www.puc.texas.gov/)).

### The Violations
By voting to pass an order engineered in darkness, the participating board members directly breached their fiduciary duties of honesty, transparency, and loyalty to the rate-paying citizens who elected them. Under Government Code § [551.141](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.141), any action taken by a governmental body in violation of the Open Meetings Act is legally **voidable**. Because the entire financial architecture of this rate order occurred within an illegal, unposted private environment, the resulting rate hike lacks legitimate legal grounding.
