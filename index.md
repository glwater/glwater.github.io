## Grand Lakes Water Districts: Action for Justice and Accountability

Dedicated to transparency and lawful governance across Grand Lakes MUDs & WCID. We expose conflicts of interest, fight consultant legal abuse, and audit fishy budgets. 
Through rigorous financial analysis of public records, we hold boards to their fiduciary duties to ensure responsible spending and protect local taxpayers from fraud.
