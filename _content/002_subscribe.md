### 📡 Stay Informed Instantly
We want you to check this site every day for water district news, alerts, or updates on spending, finances, projects, and the likes. But you don't have to, if you are busy. Automation tools will help make your job easier. By subscribing to our built-in RSS feed, our notices will drop right into your preferred application the moment they are published.

### ⏱️ The Brief Setup Guide
**<u>For Microsoft Outlook</u>:** Right-click the **RSS Subscriptions** (or *RSS Feeds*) folder in your left-hand mailbox sidebar, select **Add a New RSS Feed**, paste our portal URL [https://glwater.github.io](https://glwater.github.io), and click **Add**.

**<u>For Feedly (and mobile readers)</u>:** Open your **Feedly** application, click the **Follow Sources** button on the left navigation panel, paste our website address into the search bar, and click follow to add it to your daily news streams.

### 💡 Why Use This?
Most modern feed reader applications—including **Outlook** and **Feedly**—possess smart internal discovery engines. You don't have to hunt down specific complex code folders or backend parameters; simply dropping our core homepage link into their search or subscription utility prompts the platform to automatically crawl, track, and hook onto our live update loop in under two minutes.
