### 📋 The Qualifications: Can You Run?
The state criteria to serve as a MUD or WCID Director under the **Texas Water Code** are straightforward. You do not need a background in engineering, accounting, or law. To qualify, you must:
- Be at least **18 years old**.
- Be a **resident citizen of the State of Texas**.
- Either **own land subject to taxation** within your specific district (MUD 1, 2, 4, or WCID) OR be a **qualified registered voter** living inside that district.

### ⏱️ Key Dates & The Filing Window
Texas holds local political subdivision elections on the uniform **May election dates** (the first Saturday in May). 
* **The Filing Window for a MUD:** For a MUD, the official window to file your candidate paperwork generally opens in **mid-January** and closes firmly 30 days later in **mid-February** (exactly 78 days before election day). Election Code §§ [144.005(d)](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=EL&chapter=EL.144&artSec=144.005) and [141.040(a)(2)](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=EL&chapter=EL.141&artSec=141.040).
* **The Filing Window for a WCID:** For a WCID, the official window to file your candidate paperwork generally opens in **mid-February** and closes firmly 30 days later in **mid-March** (exactly 45 days before election day). Water Code § [51.075](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=WA&chapter=WA.51&artSec=51.075) and Election Code § [141.040(a)(2)](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=EL&chapter=EL.141&artSec=141.040). 
* **The Application:** One must submit a formal "Application for Place on the General Election Ballot" \[[Form](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/elections/forms/pol-sub/2-49f.pdf) \| [Notes](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/elections/forms/pol-sub/2-49.pdf)\] directly to the designated Board Secretary or the district's legal counsel. The official forms are accessible on the [Texas Secretary of State Candidate Portal](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/elections/candidates/index.shtml) (*see* the [Useful Links](#useful-links) section below).

### 🚀 Our Grassroots Gameplan
Running a water district campaign does not take a massive political budget; it takes ground game:
* Running on a platform of “Independent Oversight,” “Impeccable Representation,” and “Vendor Accountability” resonates across the entire neighborhood.
* **Focus on the Finances:** Neighbors are already feeling the pinch from high water bills, pass-through fees, endless projects each in the hundreds of thousands of dollars, attempted bonds that will put private consultants in control of our water districts for 40 more years, annual tax rates regularly pushed to the maximum allowable by law, rising consultant fees far beyond the inflation rate, depletion of millions of dollars in reserves through questionable practices, and much more. 
* **Coordinate the Votes:** If two or three reform candidates run as a slate for open seats, neighbors can vote for the entire package, immediately shifting the majority voting block on the board.
* **Block-Walking:** Because our districts are tightly localized, a candidate can knock on nearly every door in their water district zone within a few weekends.
