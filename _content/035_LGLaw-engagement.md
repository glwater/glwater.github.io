The adoption of the [Grand Lakes MUD No. 2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/) water rate order in late 2023 / early 2024 was not merely an issue of procedural secrecy; it involved significant administrative alterations and alarming irregularities surrounding official document execution.

### Retroactive Enforcement and Date Tampering
[Grand Lakes MUD No. 2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/) officially announced the newly adopted Rate Order on [February 19, 2024](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/posts/2024-02-19/rate-order-change-notice), wrongly and wrongfully enforcing it retroactively from February 1, 2024. Due to staggered individual billing cycles, some local households were unlawfully billed at the higher rates for services running from early January 2024. 

More concerningly, the finalized [Rate Order](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1g-eufnOms6V9jo2wpGa0DWrm3JhzEiLK/view) originally carried a formal effective date of **February 1, 2023** (*see* p. 7/54 of the order). When residents noticed this and demanded that the invalid order be nullified, district consultants took matters into their own hands without public oversight.

In a [June 4, 2024 affidavit](/assets/docs/20240604_Affidavit-MeliaBerry.pdf), paralegal Melia Berry of [Schwartz, Page & Harding](https://www.sphllp.com/) says she discovered the date discrepancy on February 25, 2024. She did not; the law firm received formal correspondence from residents on the matter. The following day, seemingly acting under instructions from attorney [Christopher Todd Skinner](https://www.sphllp.com/_chris_skinner.html), Berry altered the official document's effective date to **February 1, 2024**. This critical modification was executed and updated on the MUD website by the media management company, [Touchstone District Services](https://www.touchstonedistrictservices.com/), without:
* Any public announcement or notice to residents.
* Distributing a revised rate order to the community.
* Conducting a new public board discussion or vote.
* Re-signing or properly re-executing the altered order.

### A Forged Signature?
As residents actively mounted an appeal against the rate hike with the Public Utility Commission of Texas ([PUCT](https://www.puc.texas.gov/)), the MUD board engaged the Austin-based law firm [Lloyd Gosselink Rochelle & Townsend](https://www.lglawfirm.com/) to fight the community's protest. 

The official [engagement letter](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1k4mrYap686B3iJvO5coLovA1MqWqxREP/view) for this legal defense is dated May 13, 2024, and was signed on May 20, 2024, under the name of **Michael Christopher McClusky**, who served as the MUD board president at the time. A visual analysis of the document reveals a severe discrepancy. McClusky’s signature on the May 20, 2024 engagement letter differs widely from his verified baseline signature—extracted from his official Texas Ethics Commission [Campaign Finance Filing](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Uw1k64wrHBY1BB5ISvLbZyuboC6cktJd/view). 

The signature on the legal engagement letter could not be but fabricated, bypassing lawful board oversight. Under the Texas Penal Code, tampering with a governmental record (§ [37.10](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=PE&chapter=PE.37&artSec=37.10)) and forgery (§ [32.21](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=PE&chapter=PE.32&artSec=32.21)) carry severe criminal penalties. This paper trail raises deeply troubling questions regarding the lengths to which district consultants went to validate their actions and suppress public pushback.

### Not Coincidental
The incident of signature forgery we documented here is not coincidental at all. Our article "[Signature forgery and tampering with governmental records](./032_sig-forgery)" documented elaborate and persistent forgery and tampering with governmental records by [Schwartz, Page & Harding](https://www.sphllp.com/) staff. A thorough, independent investigation by appropriate authorities ([Harris County District Attorney's Office](https://dao.harriscountytx.gov/), [FBI Houston](https://www.fbi.gov/contact-us/field-offices/houston), [Texas Attorney General](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/), [Texas Commission on Environmental Quality](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/), and others) is warranted and urgent.
