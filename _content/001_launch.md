### 📢 Introducing the New Grand Lakes Water Districts Community Portal!
<br>
Neighbor,

We are thrilled to officially launch the Grand Lakes Water Districts Community Portal—a centralized, transparent digital resource built entirely for the 2,739 households across our three MUDs ([MUD4](https://www.grandlakesmud4.com/), [MUD1](https://www.grandlakesmud1.com/), [MUD2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/)) and [WCID](https://districtdirectory.org/fort-bend-county/grand-lakes-water-control-and-improvement-district/).

As a community, staying informed about our shared local utilities shouldn't require digging through paperwork or guessing where to find critical information. This independent portal was built to put governance, transparency, and compliance right at your fingertips.

### 🚀 The Journey Begins
Welcome to our new digital home at [glwater.github.io](https://glwater.github.io)! This platform is designed to inform the Grand Lakes community about everything related to the four water districts ([MUD4](https://www.grandlakesmud4.com/), [MUD1](https://www.grandlakesmud1.com/), [MUD2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/), and [WCID](https://districtdirectory.org/fort-bend-county/grand-lakes-water-control-and-improvement-district/)): Governance, Compliance, Accountability, Finances, Infrastructure, Consultants (attorney, operator, bookkeeper, auditor, financial advisor, engineer, tax collector, delinquent tax attorney, trash collector, web hosting provider), and more.

### 🛠️ 🎯 What We Are Building - What You Can Find on the Site<br><br>
* **📢 Alerts:** Alerts will lock cleanly at the top of the portal, and will cover all sorts of admonitions: emergencies, warnings, intelligence, tips, advisories, and notes.
* **📑 Streamlined Archives:** Easily browse news, updates, financial analysis, ongoing projects, and neighborhood alerts filtered by Tag and Category.
* **📊 Financial Transparency & Budgets:** Review breakdowns of infrastructure projects, budgets, taxes, and audits and see exactly how allocations are being utilized across our water districts.
* **🛜 Live RSS & Notification Feeds:** Subscribe to our fresh feed system to get notifications delivered instantly to your favorite reader app.
* **📖 Shared Documentation:** Clear, informative material, analyses, visualization, and guides.
* **🤝 Collaborative Ecosystem:** As this portal gets developed further, we will consider a space to submit issues, propose features, and share insights.

We want the platform to be an invaluable resource. This portal does not ask the reader to accept a conclusion. Rather, it provides a framework for examining governance. For each matter discussed, readers can consider the evidence and determine whether lawful governance, accountability, transparency, oversight, and other safeguards were present. With this, we would have followed a democratic, independent pattern: Learn → Understand → Examine → Compare → Decide.

• We will furnish the information ~ what happened.<br>
• We will document the information carefully ~ show the evidence.<br>
• We will explain the information clearly ~ identify the issues and explain why it matters.<br>
• We will present the governing law ~ what accountability requires.<br>
• We will let the reader decide.

### 🤝 This is a Community Effort!
This site is a labor of love built from the ground up to serve our neighborhood. As we scale up operations, passionate local volunteers are welcome to help keep the portal thriving—maintain site; maintain updates; contribute articles; review and edit articles; contact and interview board members; contact and interview consultants; investigate projects, finances, spending, fees; conduct analysis; write Linux scripts; write code and develop data visualization (python, R, C, html, js, markdown); design graphics (adobe, TikZ, mermaid, other); and more.

Whether you have tech skills, a knack for writing, or just want to help keep your neighbors informed, your contributions will make a difference! Take a look around, explore the portal, and bookmark the site: 👉 [glwater.github.io](https://glwater.github.io/).

### 📅 Next Steps
Documentation modules and articles are being developed on a daily basis. Check back as/when you can for more updates and news!
