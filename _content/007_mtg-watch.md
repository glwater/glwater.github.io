The Grand Lakes MUD and WCID boards meet monthly to approve invoices, budgets, tax rates, water/wastewater rates; review operations; vote on infrastructure adjustments; sign contracts; amend agreements; and much more. All of this impacts the livelihood of some 2,740 households and 40 local businesses served by the Grand Lakes Water Districts (MUD4, MUD1, MUD2, and WCID) in Katy, Fort Bend County, Texas. The public is **legally** invited to attend and speak during public comment.

⚠️ *Note: Meeting agendas must be legally posted online and physically within the district at least **three business days in advance** (Government Code § [551.043](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.043)). Always confirm the agenda before commuting.*

### 📍 Know Your Rights: Bring the Meetings Home!
Tired of driving to corporate law offices in Downtown Houston just to have your voice heard? Under Water Code § **[49.062](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=WA&chapter=WA.49&artSec=49.062)**, if five registered voters in a district petition the board, the board must designate a regular meeting location within the boundaries of the district or within 10 miles of it. Let's pool our signatures and force them to meet in Katy!
