### Collusion in Austin: How the PUCT Tainted the Grand Lakes Rate Appeal
When the households of [Grand Lakes MUD No. 2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/) realized their monthly water bills were being artificially doubled or tripled (*see* our article [The Paper Trail of an Invalid Rate Order](./035_LGLaw-engagement)), they turned to state regulators for relief. What they encountered instead was procedural failure and documented backchannel collusion.

### Gathering the Signatures
Under Texas Water Code § [13.043](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=WA&chapter=WA.13&artSec=13.043)(c), residents have a legal right to petition the Public Utility Commission of Texas ([PUCT](https://www.puc.texas.gov/)) for a formal review of predatory rate increases. The law requires a petition to be signed by the lesser of 10,000 or 10% of total ratepayers. 

Given that [Grand Lakes MUD No. 2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/) serves 682 households, local organizers needed 68 valid signatures. Community volunteers walked door-to-door and quickly gathered 92 signatures, successfully filing their official appeal on May 7, 2024. The [PUCT](https://www.puc.texas.gov/) formally logged the case as Docket No. [56589](https://interchange.puc.texas.gov/Search/Filings?ControlNumber=56589).

### Ex Parte Communications and Backchannel Collusion
Administrative review processes are legally required to remain strictly impartial. However, records reveal that the proceeding was profoundly compromised by a series of 25 ex parte email communications and a secretive phone call on May 20, 2024. 

These interactions occurred directly between [PUCT](https://www.puc.texas.gov/) Legal Division staff attorney **[Kevin Pierce](https://www.texasbar.com/AM/Template.cfm?Section=Find_A_Lawyer&template=/Customsource/MemberDirectory/MemberDirectoryDetail.cfm&ContactID=341965)**—whose public duty was to defend the public interest—and defense attorney **[Jamie Lynn Mauldin](https://www.lglawfirm.com/attorney/mauldin-jamie-l/)** of [Lloyd Gosselink Rochelle & Townsend](https://www.lglawfirm.com/), the firm hired to fight the ratepayers and protect the MUD's rate hike.

>
The records expose a deeply collaborative effort to derail the community's petition:
* **May 15, 2024:** [PUCT](https://www.puc.texas.gov/) staff attorney [Kevin Pierce](https://www.texasbar.com/AM/Template.cfm?Section=Find_A_Lawyer&template=/Customsource/MemberDirectory/MemberDirectoryDetail.cfm&ContactID=341965) initiated contact, writing to the MUD’s defense lawyer: *"I'm also happy to hop on a call to discuss this docket if there is anything y'all want to discuss at the outset."*
* **June 5, 2024:** [Pierce](https://www.texasbar.com/AM/Template.cfm?Section=Find_A_Lawyer&template=/Customsource/MemberDirectory/MemberDirectoryDetail.cfm&ContactID=341965) actively reassured defense lawyer [Jamie Lynn Mauldin](https://www.lglawfirm.com/attorney/mauldin-jamie-l/) regarding his timeline, noting: *"I hope you’ll understand me taking some additional time to make sure everything is airtight and cleared with my leadership team before filing our response."*
* **June 17, 2024:** Defense attorney [Jamie Lynn Mauldin](https://www.lglawfirm.com/attorney/mauldin-jamie-l/) sought inside information, emailing [Pierce](https://www.texasbar.com/AM/Template.cfm?Section=Find_A_Lawyer&template=/Customsource/MemberDirectory/MemberDirectoryDetail.cfm&ContactID=341965) and inflating him: *"Do you know yet how your [Subject Matter Experts] SMEs will be commenting on the petition? Give me a call to discuss if you’d like."*
* **June 17, 2024 (Later that day):** [Pierce](https://www.texasbar.com/AM/Template.cfm?Section=Find_A_Lawyer&template=/Customsource/MemberDirectory/MemberDirectoryDetail.cfm&ContactID=341965) leaked his upcoming legal strategy to the MUD defense counsel eleven days before delivering his recommendation: *"I think that we’re likely to move for dismissal and therefore not recommend a procedural schedule until the motions to dismiss are addressed."*

### The Baseless Dismissal
On August 15, 2024, following 100 days of *useless* and *wasted* review, the [PUCT](https://www.puc.texas.gov/) officially dismissed the residents' petition with prejudice. The commission claimed the appeal was filed six days past the strict 90-day statutory deadline outlined in the Texas Water Code.

This timeline calculation entirely ignored the fact that the MUD failed to [announce](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/posts/2024-02-19/rate-order-change-notice) the Rate Order on its website until February 19, 2024, and that the order was quietly and unlawfully altered on February 26, 2024; changing the effective date from February 1, 2023 to February 1, 2024. Just over a week later, on August 23, 2024, the [PUCT](https://www.puc.texas.gov/) voted to exclude the residents' Motion for Rehearing from any open meeting agenda, effectively shutting down the community's administrative avenue for justice.

### Systemic Institutional Failure
The [PUCT](https://www.puc.texas.gov/) failed its fundamental duties—Our administrative appeal was not decided on its objective merits. Instead, it was systematically undermined by documented backchannel collusion and a deliberate manipulation of regulatory timelines.

The evidence establishes a clear breakdown of legal, ethical, and administrative integrity across multiple levels:

- **Documented Ex Parte Collusion:** The strict requirement for regulatory impartiality was discarded through conscious violations by a [PUCT](https://www.puc.texas.gov/) staff attorney whose role is to protect public interests.

- **Manufactured Procedural Dismissal:** The PUCT’s ultimate dismissal of the petition on August 15, 2024—citing a six-day lapse of the 90-day statutory deadline—relied on a factually distorted timeline. The commission weaponized manufactured and baseless administrative procedures against the very citizens it is statutorily mandated to protect.

- **Institutional Shielding and Accountability Failures:** The institutional failure extended far past the initial dismissal. The [PUCT](https://www.puc.texas.gov/) actively blocked transparency and internal recourse by suppressing Public Information Act (PIA) requests with restrictive cost estimates, refusing to investigate the matter via its fraud division <mailto:fraud@puc.texas.gov>, and voting on August 23, 2024, to completely bar the residents' Motion for Rehearing from the open meeting agenda.
