The Grand Lakes community has been going through much, for years. The irregularities are serious, persistent, and involve violations of Texas open-government requirements, public-record requirements, governmental-record provisions, financial controls, contractual authority, election procedures, and other laws. 

Some matters may ultimately prove to be negligence, mismanagement, misconduct, intentional wrongdoing, misunderstandings, or administrative errors. That is precisely why an independent investigation is necessary. 

> 
A competent investigation must guide the truth-finding effort. 
- Only competent investigation can determine which is which. 
- Demanding an investigation is not the same thing as declaring someone guilty.
- The answer cannot be: “Trust us.”
- The answer must be: “Here are the records. Here is the evidence. Here is what happened. Here is who authorized it. Here is what it cost. Here is what the law requires. And here is what we are doing to correct it.”
- That is accountability.
- Grand Lakes deserves justice.
