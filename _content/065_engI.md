Across Grand Lakes [MUD No. 1](https://www.grandlakesmud1.com/), [MUD No. 2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/), and [MUD No. 4](https://www.grandlakesmud4.com/), shared water and wastewater facilities are governed by binding pro-rata ownership and capacity agreements. Yet board minutes from 2025 and 2026 reveal a troubling pattern: contract splits are routinely altered without engineering surveys, and the District’s general counsel—not the engineer—is dictating how taxpayers are billed.

### The Lift Station No. 1 Engineering Split: Ignoring Pro-Rata Ownership
According to District audit reports and Pape-Dawson’s own Bond Authorization Report, the three Grand Lakes MUDs share Wastewater Treatment Plant (WWTP) capacity on a strict pro-rata basis:
* **Grand Lakes MUD No. 4:** 49.82%
* **Grand Lakes MUD No. 1:** 29.10%
* **Grand Lakes MUD No. 2:** 21.08%

However, in January and February 2026, [Pape-Dawson](https://www.pape-dawson.com/) representative Jennifer Mays, presented separate engineering proposals for **Lift Station No. 1 Improvements** to each board—resulting in **$384,821** in combined engineering fees divided almost equally rather than by ownership share.

| District | Meeting Date | Approved Fee | Actual Share Paid | Contractual WWTP Share | Overpayment / Variance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MUD No. 4** | Feb 18, 2026 | $133,491 | 34.69% | 49.82% | -$58,227 (Underbilled) |
| **MUD No. 1** | Dec 1, 2025 | $126,310 | 32.82% | 29.10% | +$14,327 (Overbilled) |
| **MUD No. 2** | Feb 2, 2026 | $125,020 | 32.49% | 21.08% | +$43,900 (Overbilled) |
| **Total** | — | **$384,821** | **100%** | **100%** | — |

By presenting siloed proposals to three separate boards—where MUD 2 directors do not see what MUD 4 directors are told—MUD 2 taxpayers were billed **$125,020** instead of their 21.08% pro-rata share (**$81,120**), an immediate **$43,900 overcharge** on engineering fees alone.

### The Peek & Fry Road Waterline: When the Attorney Becomes the Surveyor
A similar breakdown in governance occurred on the **Peek Road and Fry Road Water Line Replacement Project**. Initially estimated at **$462,361**, construction was awarded to [ARS Construction](https://arsconstruct.com/) for **$307,725** plus **$58,100** in [Pape-Dawson](https://www.pape-dawson.com/) construction phase services.

In the April 21, 2025 MUD No. 2 Board Meeting ([**Minutes**](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/static/b822e8ac1d391d5c5a836b8146e348cd/Minutes_April_4_2025_amended_18bd95184e.pdf)), directors questioned why MUD 2 was being forced to pay **50%** of the shared project costs while MUD 1 and MUD 4 paid **25%** each—despite MUD 2 holding only **21.55%** of water plant capacity. Rather than the District engineer presenting a certified boundary survey or linear-footage calculation, MUD attorney [Christopher Todd Skinner](https://www.sphllp.com/_chris_skinner) ([Schwartz, Page & Harding](https://www.sphllp.com/)) stepped in to settle the question verbally, claiming that *"fifty percent of the area where there will be pipe replaced is within the District [MUD 2], and the remaining fifty percent of the area is split evenly between [MUD 4 and MUD 1]."* Who is this attorney and what is his role? How did he know this information? Is there any credibility or legitimacy to his claim? Is his claim supported by a signed, sealed survey? Has he been in contact with the engineer in the background? Has there been attorney-engineer background coordination? What is the plan and what are they trying to achieve?

### Unanswered Governance Questions
- **Why is legal counsel determining engineering quantities?** Calculating linear feet of pipe across jurisdictional boundaries is a licensed surveying and engineering function, not legal advice.
- **Where is the boundary exhibit?** No signed, sealed survey exhibit showing pipe footage per district was attached to substantiate the 50/25/25 split.
- **Why did boards accept verbal assurances?** Across all three districts, directors approved hundreds of thousands of dollars in public expenditures without demanding written contractual justification.
