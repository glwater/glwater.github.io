### The Silent Cancellation of the 2026 Directors’ Election
For forty years, localized legal representation has quietly steered the Grand Lakes water infrastructure. But the events leading up to May 2, 2026, exposed a severe structural manipulation that stripped the Grand Lakes community—comprising 2,740 households and 40 businesses—of their constitutional right to vote.

The terms for three [Grand Lakes Water Control & Improvement District (WCID)](https://districtdirectory.org/fort-bend-county/grand-lakes-water-control-and-improvement-district/) directors (Andrew R. Reed, Gina D’Agostino, and Matthew Froehlich) were set to expire in May 2026. Instead of holding a democratic election, outside legal counsel ([Gordon Christopher Cranner](https://www.sphllp.com/_gordon_cranner.html) of [Schwartz, Page & Harding](https://www.sphllp.com/)) quietly executed a cancellation order on March 26, 2026, under the baseless claim that the incumbents were unopposed by February 17, 2026.

### The Statutory Mismatch: WCID vs. MUD
The legal framework relies entirely on exploiting the community's lack of awareness regarding the different chapters of the Texas Water Code.

* **Water Control & Improvement Districts (WCIDs):** Governed strictly by [Chapter 51](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=WA&chapter=WA.51&artSec=). By law, the candidacy window for a May 2 election must span from February 16 to March 18, 2026.
* **Municipal Utility Districts (MUDs):** Governed by [Chapter 54](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=WA&chapter=WA.54&artSec=). These entities utilize an earlier filing window that would span from January 14 to February 13, 2026.

[Cranner](https://www.sphllp.com/_gordon_cranner.html) published a single physical notice at a water plant situated at *5420 South Fry Road* (Fort Bend County voter precinct 1133). This notice unlawfully forced a MUD-based timeline (January 14 to February 13) onto a WCID entity. By enforcing an artificial, premature deadline and neglecting mandated physical postings in voter precincts 1132 and 1144, the public was systemically locked out of filing for candidacies. The election was subsequently canceled under the premise that no challengers stepped forward by February 17, 2026.

### Systemic Concealment of Public Information
Uncovering these structural maneuvers required aggressive use of Public Information Requests. Investigative tracking has confirmed that critical regulatory transparency protocols were completely bypassed:

- **No digital paper trail:** No official election orders, sample ballots, or cancellation orders were ever hosted on the [WCID's designated public web directory](https://districtdirectory.org/fort-bend-county/grand-lakes-water-control-and-improvement-district/).
- **Missing sample ballot and text:** Official inquiries revealed the district held zero communications with Fort Bend County regarding ballot text and never drafted a sample layout, shared it with the county, or published it on the district's website.
- **Empty schedules:** The formal January election order was approved with **completely blank exhibits** for early voting and Election Day polling locations.
- **Notice without order:** The December 11, 2025, notice of election was published without an election order. The election order was passed and adopted 42 days later, on January 22, 2026. 
- **Defiance of the law:** The [Schwartz, Page & Harding](https://www.sphllp.com/) attorneys have reached a level of disconnection, denial, and defiance that they would comfortably state that Texas state laws do not legally compel them to actively broadcast resolutions / orders or maintain structural clarity on the district portal.
