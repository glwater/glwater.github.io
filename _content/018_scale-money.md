The numbers alone justify public scrutiny.

For fiscal year 2025, general-fund expenditures as shown in the financial reports total $11.16 million for the four Grand Lakes water districts.

- Grand Lakes MUD No. 2 — $3.98 million.
- Grand Lakes MUD No. 1 — $3.26 million.
- Grand Lakes MUD No. 4 — $3.57 million.
- Grand Lakes WCID — $346,000.

Those expenditures include millions of dollars for water and wastewater operations, repairs, contracted services, security, trash disposal, legal services, engineering, auditing, capital expenditures, and administration.

Over the past 25 years, it is estimated that the four districts have processed more than $275 million in public expenditures. This is not pocket change.

Even modest inefficiencies, conflicts of interest, unnecessary projects, excessive professional fees, or failures of financial controls can become enormously expensive when repeated year after year. And once money is spent, it is often impossible to recover. That is why the community must ask questions before another generation of expenditures, contracts, rate increases, or debt becomes irreversible.
