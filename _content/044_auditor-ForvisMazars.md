### Why it matters
For fiscal year 2025, general-fund expenditures for the four Grand Lakes water districts totaled $11.16 million. It is then estimated that over the past 25 years, the four districts have processed more than $275 million in public expenditures. This is not pocket change; the numbers justify public scrutiny. Our article "[The scale of the money is enormous](./018_scale-money)" said it all:

Even modest inefficiencies, conflicts of interest, unnecessary projects, excessive professional fees, or failures of financial controls can become enormously expensive when repeated year after year. And once money is spent, it is often impossible to recover. That is why the community must ask questions before another generation of expenditures, contracts, rate increases, or debt becomes irreversible.
{: .flash2}

### The Grand Lakes water district auditor 
[Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.us/) represents the consolidation of BKD (acquired 2022) and Mazars U.S. (merged 2024).

[Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.us/) has acted as the auditor for our Grand Lakes water districts ([MUD1](https://www.grandlakesmud1.com/), [MUD2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/), [MUD4](https://www.grandlakesmud4.com/), and [WCID](https://districtdirectory.org/fort-bend-county/grand-lakes-water-control-and-improvement-district/)) since their respective inclusions (GLWCID in 1990, GLMUD4 in 1993, GLMUD2 in 2001, and GLMUD1 in 2002).

Sherri Greenwood, a partner at [Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.us/), got certified in March 1991 (over 35 years ago). Having attended the Grand Lakes water district board meetings for many years, she should have sensed irregularities. She should have raised concerns and reported such. She chose not to.

Greenwood and her employer cannot invoke confusion, negligence, or ignorance. When maintaining silence on any irregularity, or suspicion of so, the consultants serving our water districts cause grave and irreparable harm to 2,740 households and 40 businesses in the Grand Lakes community. 

### TSBPA opens an investigation
Following a formal complaint submitted by our community to the [Texas State Board of Public Accountancy (TSBPA)](https://www.tsbpa.texas.gov/), the TSBPA opened an investigative file (26-10-12T) on October 20, 2026. The investigation focuses on auditor [Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.us/).

We will not comment further on this matter until the auditor responds to the complaint and the case formally moves before the responsible [TSBPA](https://www.tsbpa.texas.gov/) Committee. We must, however, note that absence of accountability is destructive to a modern, democratic system of governance. 

### Transparency
All four Grand Lakes water districts ([MUD4](https://www.grandlakesmud4.com/), [MUD1](https://www.grandlakesmud1.com/), [MUD2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/), and [WCID](https://districtdirectory.org/fort-bend-county/grand-lakes-water-control-and-improvement-district/)) must add this information to the monthly board meeting minutes, mentioning the consultant and the [TSBPA](https://www.tsbpa.texas.gov/) case file number. In failing or refusing to do so, the boards and attorneys [Christopher Todd Skinner](https://www.sphllp.com/_chris_skinner.html) / [Gordon Christopher Cranner](https://www.sphllp.com/_gordon_cranner.html) of [Schwartz, Page & Harding](https://www.sphllp.com/) will be consciously involved in covering up state investigations and concealing crucial information from the public.
