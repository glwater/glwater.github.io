### 🤝 Neighbors Helping Neighbors
The Grand Lakes Community Portal is an independent oversight project run entirely by local volunteers. To maintain transparency, push timely alerts, and deliver accurate data to our **2,739 households**, we need a dedicated team behind the scenes. 

Whether you can spare 1 hour a week or want to help lead a project, there is a place for you here. Passionate local volunteers are welcome to help keep the portal thriving—maintain site; maintain updates; contribute articles; review and edit articles; contact and interview board members; contact and interview consultants; investigate projects, finances, spending, fees; conduct analysis; write Linux scripts; write code and develop data visualization (python, R, C, html, js, markdown); design graphics (adobe, TikZ, mermaid, gnuplot, other); and more. Whether you have tech skills, a knack for writing, or just want to help keep your neighbors informed, your contributions will make a difference! Take a look around, explore the site, and see if you can volunteer and contribute...

### 🛠️ Available Volunteer Roles
We have broken down our needs into specific functional areas. Choose a role that fits your interest and skill set:

* **District Observers / Public Access Coordinators / Field Representatives**  
*Attend MUD/WCID monthly board meetings, listen in, question the board and consultants, file public information requests, follow up, and report back to the project.*

* **Financial & Compliance Analysts / Regulatory, Legal, Financial Fraud Investigators**  
*Examine budgets, audit reports, engagement letters, agreements, consultant fees, proposed projects, and monthly MUD / WCID board meeting agendas and minutes for discrepancies, issues, or unlawful practices. Document non-compliances with the Texas Water Code, Government Code, Tax Code, and Election Code. Escalate to the project.*

* **Legal & Policy Advisors**  
  *Review documented non-compliances, verify potential unlawful practices flagged by Analysts, and advise on formal escalation or official reporting to state regulatory agencies.*

* **District & Open Government Content Contributors**  
  *Take the raw data and legal findings and translate them into clean, plain-language summaries and articles for the community.. Forward to Proofreaders.*
  
* **Quality Assurance & Verifiers (Proofreaders)**  
  *Review financial data, citations, project comments, and contributor summaries for typos, clarity, and verifiability before publication.*
  
* **Community Outreach & Engagement**  
  *Help share official portal update links onto local platforms like Nextdoor or Facebook groups to reach less connected residents.*
  
* **Data Visualization & Technical Designers**  
  *Meaningfully grow your open-source, Linux, and development portfolio. Help build impactful data visualizations using Python, R, C, HTML/JS, or gnuplot. Design clean technical graphics using Markdown, TikZ, Mermaid, or Adobe suites. Develop automated tracking scripts.*

* **Technical Infrastructure & DevOps Maintainers**  
  *Format articles for publication, handle email newsletters, automate data scraping, develop tracking scripts, ensure the technical infrastructure is secure and running smoothly, and synchronize the GitHub Portal, Facebook Page, and X Profile.*
