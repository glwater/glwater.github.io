🚨 Approximately $46 million in misallocated sales tax revenue (~$23M each) flowed to [Grand Lakes MUD No. 2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/) and the [City of Houston](https://houstontx.gov/) due to [The Home Depot](https://www.homedepot.com/) reporting errors. When tens of millions of dollars in public funds are spent without proper oversight, state and local authorities have a clear legal duty to step in—otherwise, public accountability and the rule of law mean nothing.

We are calling for an immediate, transparent investigation into the handling, oversight, and expenditure of these funds across all parties involved:

- The [City of Houston](https://houstontx.gov/).  
- [Grand Lakes MUD No. 2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/) and its Board of Directors (Michael Christopher McClusky, John Scot Vann, William Donald Butler, Gregory Jay Henry, and Bradley Allen Baker).  
- Legal counsel [Schwartz, Page & Harding](https://www.sphllp.com/) and [Christopher Todd Skinner](https://www.sphllp.com/_chris_skinner).  
- Bookkeeper [Municipal Accounts & Consulting](https://municipalaccounts.com/) and Taylor Watson.  
- Auditor [Forvis Mazars US](https://www.forvismazars.us/) (Houston office audit team).  
- Any other vendors or parties who benefited from these funds.

Public trust depends on enforcement. We urge immediate action from the agencies tasked with protecting Texas taxpayers:

⚖️ [Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts](https://comptroller.texas.gov/): Audit and trace the misallocated sales tax revenue.  
⚖️ [State Auditor's Office](https://sao.texas.gov/): Audit the financial transactions of the district for waste, financial mismanagement, improper vendor billing, and unauthorized expenditures.  
⚖️ [Harris County District Attorney's Office](https://dao.harriscountytx.gov/): Investigate potential misuse of public funds.  
⚖️ [Texas Department of Public Safety – Southeast Texas Region (Texas Rangers)](https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/texas-rangers): Conduct an independent public integrity review.

📌 Taxpayers deserve answers—and accountability under the law.
