Under Texas law, MUDs and WCIDs are public entities. Every contract, email, invoice, and vote is public record. Use this template and guide to start auditing them.

### Tips for Grand Lakes Residents: How to File a Public Information Request
**The 10-Day Rule:** By law, the water district must produce public records "promptly," or notify you within 10 business days if they are seeking an opinion from the Texas Attorney General to withhold them.

**Keep it Digital:** Always specify that you want records sent via email or an online portal to avoid a cost estimate (administrative fees); a reprehensible, unlawful tactic the [Schwartz, Page & Harding](https://www.sphllp.com/) attorneys employ to avoid or delay the production of public information.

**The Operator Trap:** If you ask the private service firm directly, they may ignore you. Always submit the request to the official MUD board's designated public information coordinator (usually the district's legal counsel or their official office address).

### Public Records Request Template
Adapt the template below, fill in the brackets, and email it directly to < <mailto: glmud2PIA@sphllp.com> >, < <mailto: glmud4PIA@sphllp.com> >, < <mailto: glmud1PIA@sphllp.com> >, or < <mailto: glwcidPIA@sphllp.com> >. It is noted that the WCID serves the entire Grand Lakes community, regardless of which MUD serves you.

<blockquote markdown="1">
<b>Subject:</b> Texas Public Information Act Request – Grand Lakes WCID (or MUD No. x)

The Public Information Coordinator,

I am a resident of Grand Lakes and am formally requesting access to and copies of the following public records under the Texas Public Information Act (Texas Government Code, Chapter 552):

- A copy of the current, active master service agreement/contract between Grand Lakes MUD No. x and its primary private utility operator/service firm.
- Itemized invoices, billing statements, and proof of payment issued to said private utility operator for the previous 12 months.
- Conflict of interest questionnaires (Form CIQ or Form CIS) filed by current board members and district vendors within the last two calendar years.

To minimize administrative overhead, please provide the requested records in an electronic format (PDF) to the email address listed below. And if any portion of this request is denied or withheld, please cite the specific statutory exception, notify me, and request an Office of the Attorney General ruling within the legally mandated 10-business-day window.

Thank you for your time and transparency.

Sincerely,

[Name]  
[Address]  
[Email Address]
</blockquote>
