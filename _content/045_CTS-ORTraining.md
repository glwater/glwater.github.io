### Persistent violations by the attorney serving the Grand Lakes water districts
Attorney [Christopher Todd Skinner](https://www.sphllp.com/_chris_skinner.html) has administered the four Grand Lakes water districts ([MUD1](https://www.grandlakesmud1.com/), [MUD2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/), [MUD4](https://www.grandlakesmud4.com/), and [WCID](https://districtdirectory.org/fort-bend-county/grand-lakes-water-control-and-improvement-district/)) since he joined the law firm [Schwartz, Page & Harding](https://www.sphllp.com/) in 2003.

Our article "[Questions Concerning Agendas, Minutes, and Governmental Authority](./021_access-records)" documented some of Skinner's endless violations of the [Open Meetings Act](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=) and [Public Information Act](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.552&artSec=).

- The attorney would unlawfully and unilaterally add items to the monthly meeting agendas when the minutes for the prior monthly meeting unmistakably read: "<u>FUTURE AGENDA ITEMS</u>: The Board considered matters to be placed on future Board meeting agendas and noted that there were no additional items other than those set forth hereinabove."

- The attorney issues deficient, vague, or misleading minutes that do not accurately document what occurs in the board meetings. The objective is to conceal information.

- To keep the residents / taxpayers in the dark and prevent them from uncovering and challenging wrongdoing, exhibits associated with board meeting minutes are routinely unavailable. This unlawful practice has been ongoing for many years. The [Schwartz, Page & Harding](https://www.sphllp.com/) attorneys reference a number of exhibits in the board meeting minutes, but they never attach the exhibits to the published minutes, essentially making the useless minutes they draft even more useless.

- The attorney refuses to disclose information related to the water district's finances, contracts, legal fees, infrastructure projects, etc. As this refusal is challenged via public information complaints to the [Office of the Attorney General](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov), the attorney manages to withhold public information for 12-16 months, essentially defying the very essence of Open Government.

### Disciplinary action by the Texas Attorney General
After much tampering, denial, defiance, indifference, arbitrariness, and disregard for the law, the [Office of the Attorney General](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov) has finally ordered [Christopher Todd Skinner](https://www.sphllp.com/_chris_skinner.html) of [Schwartz, Page & Harding](https://www.sphllp.com/) to complete Open Records Training and provide a completion certificate within six months from October 22, 2026. This is what the [OAG](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov) wrote in the two orders ([OR-25-057172-IC](/assets/docs/20261022_OR25057172IC.pdf) and [OR-26-007846-IC](/assets/docs/20261022_OR26007846IC.pdf)) of October 22, 2026: 
>
Further, the district’s public information officer or the officer’s designee is required to complete open records training at the following link, and provide this office with a certificate of completion, not later than six months after the date of this letter [October 22, 2026]: [PIA Training](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/open-government/governmental-bodies/pia-and-oma-training-resources/public-information-act-training). *See id.* § 552.328(c)(1).

In so doing, the OAG is telling a lawless attorney: Enough is enough! In fact, in hanging on to the trio, [Christopher Todd Skinner](https://www.sphllp.com/_chris_skinner.html) / [Gordon Christopher Cranner](https://www.sphllp.com/_gordon_cranner.html) / Melia Anne Berry, [Schwartz, Page & Harding](https://www.sphllp.com/) and its founder and managing partner, [Joseph M. Schwartz](https://www.sphllp.com/_joseph_schwartz.html), are making the wrong and costly bet!

### Transparency
All four Grand Lakes water districts ([MUD4](https://www.grandlakesmud4.com/), [MUD1](https://www.grandlakesmud1.com/), [MUD2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/), and [WCID](https://districtdirectory.org/fort-bend-county/grand-lakes-water-control-and-improvement-district/)) must add this information to the monthly board meeting minutes. In failing or refusing to do so, the boards and the attorneys serving the Grand Lakes water districts ([Christopher Todd Skinner](https://www.sphllp.com/_chris_skinner.html) / [Gordon Christopher Cranner](https://www.sphllp.com/_gordon_cranner.html)) will be consciously involved in concealing crucial information from the public and covering up OAG disciplinary actions against [Schwartz, Page & Harding](https://www.sphllp.com/) staff.
