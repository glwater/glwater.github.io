Neighbors and homeowners... We are providing you with a template that you can use or adapt to request your board members furnish details on your district's financial situation and figures: revenue, expenditures, consultant fees, bids, contracts, etc. We encourage you to take this opportunity seriously and get involved. We will secure lawful governance of our water districts only when we show our boards and the different consultants that we are monitoring, we know, we see, we understand, we care, we will question, we can scrutinize, we deserve better, we reject irregularities, we will report, and we will bring to justice... 

<blockquote markdown="1">
**Subject:** Inquiry Regarding Professional Consultant Fees and Expenditure Transparency – [Insert District Name here, e.g., Grand Lakes MUD x]

Dear Board of Directors,

As a resident, homeowner, and taxpayer within [*Insert District Name here, e.g., Grand Lakes MUD x*], I am writing to express my appreciation for your service as our elected board members. I recognize that managing our local infrastructure requires balancing many complex moving parts, and I value your dedication as community servants.

In reviewing our district’s recent financial reports and public trends, I have been looking closely at our operational expenditures. Because professional services—such as legal counsel, engineering, and financial advisory roles—are exempt from standard competitive bidding laws under Texas state procurement rules, our board holds a unique and heavy fiduciary responsibility to ensure that taxpayers are receiving fair-market value.

To help our community better understand our district's financial landscape and to ensure complete transparency, I am formally requesting a summary or breakdown of the following information:

**Current Consultant Contracts:** What are the names of the primary legal, engineering, management, and financial advisory firms currently under contract with the district, and how long has each firm consistently served us?

**Fee Structures:** Are these professional firms compensated via flat monthly retainers, hourly rates, or other arrangements ? Are these firms compensated a percentage of bond costs? Please detail.

**Annual Expenditures:** What was the total dollar amount paid out to each of these third-party consulting firms during the last fiscal year (ended July/August)?

**Market Re-evaluation:** When was the last time the board issued a Request for Proposal (RFP) / Request for Quotation (RFQ) or actively shopped around to compare our current professional service rates against other firms in the market?

As constituents, we rely on the board to be our first line of defense against the rising costs of district business. Total transparency regarding these expenses goes a long way in building trust and keeping our community well-informed.

Thank you for your time, your transparency, and your continued commitment to safeguarding our local tax dollars. I look forward to your response.

Sincerely,

[Name]  
[Property Address / Neighborhood]  
[Contact Information]
</blockquote>
