Grand Lakes residents are not asking for special treatment. They have rights, and respecting those rights is obligatory; it is the law.

We are asking for the same standards that any responsible organization should demand when managing hundreds of millions of dollars over decades:

#### Transparency
  Complete and timely access to public records, contracts, invoices, agendas, minutes, exhibits and financial information.

#### Independent oversight
  No consultant should effectively supervise, audit, approve or control its own work.

#### Competitive and reasonable costs
  Major expenditures should be justified and, where appropriate, competitively evaluated.

#### Conflict-of-interest protection
  Professional relationships among the attorney, operator, engineer, bookkeeper, financial advisor and other vendors should be disclosed and independently reviewed.

#### Accurate governmental records
  Board minutes, resolutions, orders, affidavits, contracts and other governmental records must accurately reflect what happened and who authorized it.

#### Responsible financial management
  Reserves should be protected. Waste should be eliminated before rates, taxes, or debt are increased.

#### Genuine public participation
 Residents must receive sufficient notice and meaningful information to understand and participate in decisions affecting their property and finances.

#### Accountability
  When credible evidence suggests that laws, contracts, governmental procedures, or fiduciary obligations may have been violated, the matter should be investigated by competent independent authorities.
