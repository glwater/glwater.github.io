### Family Mission: The Grand Lakes Civics Secret Code!
Hey Grand Lakes Families! Looking for a fun weekend activity that turns your kitchen table into a detective headquarters? We have a brand-new **MUD / WCID Open Government & Accountability Crossword Puzzle** ready for your junior sleuths! 

This isn't just an ordinary word game—it's a secret map that shows exactly how our neighborhood works, where our water comes from, and how local community leaders protect our neck of the woods here in Katy, Texas.

### 🚀 How to Play: The Kitchen Table Challenge
### 🕵️‍♂️ For Younger Kids: Become a Community Detective!
**Calling all Grand Lakes Secret Agents!** 🌟 

Pack away the tablets and gather the family! Did you know that underneath our streets, parks, and pools is a hidden network run by local leaders? They make big decisions about our neighborhood (water, taxes, engineering, infrastructure, and more) every single day!

We need **YOUR** help to crack the **Community Accountability Code**. Grab a pencil, team up with your parents, and track down the words hidden in the puzzle. Look closely at the clues—some words will teach you how community leaders protect our neighborhood, while others show what happens when people stop paying attention. Consider it your first official neighborhood mission! 

Turn this puzzle into a fun team game with these easy steps:

* **Team Up:** Pair older kids with younger siblings, or team up as parents vs. kids. 
* **Hunt for Clues:** Read the definitions together. Ask big questions like: *"What is a Tax Rate?"* or *"What does it mean to have Integrity?"*
* **Crack the Code:** Use the **Word Bank Checklist** at the bottom of the puzzle page to help fill in the blanks when you get stuck. 

### 🏫 Take it to School & Share the Fun!
Civics is way more fun when you learn it with friends! 
* **Classroom Challenge:** Share a copy of the puzzle with your child's teachers or local school clubs. It makes a fantastic, real-world educational activity for history, government, or social studies classes!
* **Neighborhood Research:** Once your kids crack the words, have them look up our local district online to see these words in action. Finding out who our local "Board Directors" are is like unmasking the neighborhood superheroes!
