Over a multi-year span, the legal fees [Schwartz, Page & Harding](https://www.sphllp.com/) charged to [Grand Lakes MUD No. 2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/) grew exponentially.

Under Water Code § [49.067(a)](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=WA&chapter=WA.49&artSec=49.067), a district shall contract, and be contracted with, in the name of the district. And under Water Code § [49.066(a)](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=WA&chapter=WA.49&artSec=49.066), a district may sue and be sued in the courts of the state in the name of the district by and through its board; **not** by or through its attorney. A Municipal Utility District (MUD) is a local government–a political subdivision of the state–whose business can only by executed by its board (Water Code § [49.057(a)](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=WA&chapter=WA.49&artSec=49.057)) in noticed and minuted public, open meetings (Government Code §§ [551.021](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.021), [552.041](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.041), [551.042](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.042), [551.043](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.043)); never by its attorney fantasizing in their office, home, or car about how to conduct public business to shield his private violations.
