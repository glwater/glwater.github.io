We, the residents and taxpayers, have an unconditional obligation to fight the unlawful overreach of said attorneys, prevent them from further tampering with the Grand Lakes water districts, stop their long-lasting violations, and force them to respect the law, stop discarding public money (our money), and protect–not abuse–taxpayer rights.

Water district (MUD, WCID, or other) board members have **no** power or authority to donate money to any entity. The water district board is charged with managing infrastructure projects and must discharge their roles per the Water Code and other Texas statutes, **none** of which entitles board members to approve monetary donations to schools or other entities.

The Grand Lakes residents pay much money in taxes to the [Katy ISD (Independent School District)](https://www.katyisd.org/). This is the entity the Roosevelt Alexander Elementary School Parent-Teacher Association ([Roosevelt Alexander Elementary PTA](https://www.raepta.org/)) may contact for any donations to fund school maintenance and renovation projects.

For a district with $3 million in annual budget, a $25,000 to $250,000 donation equals 0.83% to 8.3% of the annual budget.
