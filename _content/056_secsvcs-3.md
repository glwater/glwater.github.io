In here, we expand on the "Additional Security (Sheriff and Constable)" contracts we discussed in [Part I](./054_secsvcs-1) and [Part II](./055_secsvcs-2). How do $1.1 million-a-year "Additional Security (Sheriff and Constable)" contracts keep getting renewed across Grand Lakes [MUD No. 1](https://www.grandlakesmud1.com/), [MUD No. 2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/), and [MUD No. 4](https://www.grandlakesmud4.com/)? Behind closed doors, over an unposted committee table, and at a private restaurant dinner later rebranded as a "campaign event."

Newly analyzed meeting minutes, [Texas Public Information Act (TPIA)](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.552&artSec=) responses, and [Texas Attorney General (OAG)](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/) filings expose a coordinated timeline showing how Grand Lakes water district directors, their legal counsel ([Christopher Todd Skinner](https://www.sphllp.com/_chris_skinner) of [Schwartz, Page & Harding](https://www.sphllp.com/)), and Fort Bend County Precinct 1 officials ([Commissioner Vincent Morales](https://www.fortbendcountytx.gov/government/departments/commissioners-court/commissioner-precinct-1)) bypassed Texas open-government mandates—and why the matter is now headed to state ethics and criminal oversight authorities.

### The Timeline: Behind Closed Doors in Spring 2025
* **April 21, 2025 — The Unnoticed Committee Announced:** During a regular meeting of Grand Lakes MUD No. 2 ([Minutes](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/static/b822e8ac1d391d5c5a836b8146e348cd/Minutes_April_4_2025_amended_18bd95184e.pdf), p. 6), Board President Michael Christopher McClusky announces an upcoming May 14, 2025 "Advisory Committee" meeting at [Municipal District Services (MDS)](https://mdswater.com/) *"to discuss future security coverage."*
* **May 1, 2025 — The Carrabba’s Dinner:** Just 13 days before that scheduled security meeting, Fort Bend County Precinct 1 [Commissioner Vincent Morales](https://www.fortbendcountytx.gov/government/departments/commissioners-court/commissioner-precinct-1) hosts a dinner at Carrabba’s Italian Grill in Katy attended by board members from across the Grand Lakes water districts (MUDs 1, 2, 4, and WCID).
* **May 14, 2025 — The Secret Security Meeting:** The multi-district Advisory Committee convenes at [MDS](https://mdswater.com/) in Katy regarding the $1.1M Additional Sheriff and Constable Security contract. Despite deliberating on public business and multi-district taxpayer expenditures, the meeting is held with **no public notice** (Texas Government Code §§ [551.041](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.041), [551.043](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.043)) and **no public minutes or certified agenda** (§§ [551.021](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.021), [551.022](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.022)).
* **June 17, 2025 — "No Documents Responsive":** When pressed under the [TPIA](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.552&artSec=) for records showing when the Advisory Committee was formed, who sits on it, or any founding documents, MUD general counsel [Christopher Todd Skinner](https://www.sphllp.com/_chris_skinner) ([Schwartz, Page & Harding](https://www.sphllp.com/)) responds that the District has *"no documents responsive."*
* **June 24, 2025 — Rebranding Dinner as a "Campaign Event" (OAG File [OR2025-030262](/assets/docs/20250821_OAG030262.pdf)):** To block release of records surrounding the May 1 dinner, the [Fort Bend County Attorney’s Office](https://www.fortbendcountytx.gov/government/departments/county-attorney) informs the [Attorney General](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/) that the Carrabba’s gathering was a *"campaign event"* paid for with *"personal campaign funds,"* organized by a current Fort Bend County employee acting as the *"campaign's event coordinator,"* featuring guest speakers addressing *"matters concerning the public such as theft."* Notably, [Commissioner Morales](https://www.fortbendcountytx.gov/government/departments/commissioners-court/commissioner-precinct-1) had just begun his new four-year term on January 1, 2025, with no active election campaign underway.
* **August 14, 2025 — OAG Ruling [OR2025-029223](/assets/docs/20250814_OAG029223.pdf) — Withholding Information:** Drafted by Assistant Attorney General Hunter O. Simmons, the [OAG](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/) ruling permits [Grand Lakes MUD No. 2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/) to withhold internal communications under attorney-client privilege (Texas Government Code § [552.107](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.552&artSec=552.107)), relying strictly on the District’s unverified representations. When dinner, salad, pasta, fish, and wine become the subject of "rendition of professional legal services" and "attorney-client privilege", an independent, rational observer will rule that *something fishy* is going on!

### Statutory Violations at Issue
When a governmental body delegates public policy deliberations—such as structuring a $1.1M+ law enforcement contract—to a multi-district committee or gathers a quorum outside public view, Texas law provides strict transparency guardrails:

* **Texas Open Meetings Act (TOMA) Violations:** Conducting a multi-district committee meeting on May 14 without three-business-day posted notice (Texas Government Code §§ [551.041](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.041), [551.043](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.043)) or keeping minutes/recordings (§§ [551.021](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.021), [551.022](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.022)), alongside potential walking-quorum or circumvention violations under § [551.143](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.551&artSec=551.143).
* **Texas Public Information Act (TPIA) Obstruction (Texas Government Code § [552.353](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.552&artSec=552.353)):** Claiming zero foundational or membership records exist for an officially announced Advisory Committee while shielding related deliberations behind § [552.107](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=GV&chapter=GV.552&artSec=552.107) privilege claims.
* **Campaign Finance & Public Resource Questions:** Using a sitting County employee to coordinate an alleged "campaign event" four months into a Commissioner's new term—where public security contracts were the core topic—raises direct questions regarding prohibited use of public resources and accurate campaign expenditure reporting.

### Escalating to State & County Oversight
Because water district consultants and local officials refuse to release the underlying records voluntarily, the Grand Lakes Community Portal has initiated formal oversight actions. To prevent [Skinner](https://www.sphllp.com/_chris_skinner) - [Morales](https://www.fortbendcountytx.gov/government/departments/commissioners-court/commissioner-precinct-1) from coordinating efforts to undermine investigations, we will not discuss this matter further. But we will provide future updates.
