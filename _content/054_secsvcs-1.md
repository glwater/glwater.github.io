As part of our ongoing series examining how reserves across the Grand Lakes water districts have been drawn down, we examined three years of audited expenditures for **Additional Security (Sheriff and Constable) Services** across **[Grand Lakes MUD No. 1](https://www.grandlakesmud1.com/), [MUD No. 2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/), and [MUD No. 4](https://www.grandlakesmud4.com/)**.

Residents already fund standard law enforcement protection (Sheriff and Constable security services) through their annual Fort Bend County General Fund taxes and Katy ISD taxes ([Katy ISD Police](https://www.katyisd.org/police/home)). Yet audit reports show that between FY2023 and FY2025, the three Grand Lakes MUDs spent **$3,163,026** combined on *additional* security (Sheriff and Constable) contracts.

### The Disproportionate Burden on MUD No. 2
Despite serving the fewest households of the three districts, [Grand Lakes MUD No. 2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/) paid more for additional security between FY2023 and FY2025 than [MUD No. 1](https://www.grandlakesmud1.com/) and [MUD No. 4](https://www.grandlakesmud4.com/) combined:

| District | Residences | Businesses | Land (acres) | FY'23–FY'25 Security Spending | Share of Total |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **GLMUD No. 2** | 682 | 10 | 477 | **$1,793,010** | **56.7%** |
| **GLMUD No. 1** | 1,072 | 3 | 393 | **$714,788** | **22.6%** |
| **GLMUD No. 4** | 985 | 25 | 520 | **$655,228** | **20.7%** |
| **Total** | **2,739** | **38** | **1,390** | **$3,163,026** | **100%** |

*(Source: District Audit Reports, FY2023–FY2025)*

### An Abrupt 70% Spike After Debt Payoff
Historical audit figures reveal that MUD No. 2's security spending remained between $132,460 and $355,196 annually from FY2018 through FY2022. Once [MUD No. 2](https://www.grandlakesmud2.com/) paid off its debt in 2022, security expenditures jumped by **70%**—from $355,196 in FY2022 to $602,302 in FY2023—and remained near $600,000 annually through FY2025.

### Putting $1.1M Per Year Into Perspective
Grand Lakes spans roughly 2.17 square miles (1,390 acres)—just **0.25%** of Fort Bend County’s 861.8-square-mile total area. Paying **$1.1 million per year** for supplemental policing across 0.25% of the county scales to **$440 million countywide**—which exceeds Fort Bend County’s entire FY2024 General Fund budget of **$397.8 million** (per the [County's FY2023 Audit Report](https://transparencydocs.fortbendcountytx.gov/Traditional_Finances/2024/FY2023_ACFR.pdf), p. 13).
