The Grand Lakes water districts are governmental entities. They are **not** private corporations owned by their consultants.

Yet for more than two decades, the same or closely associated private firms have provided many of the essential professional services to the four Grand Lakes water districts, including legal services, water-system operations, engineering, bookkeeping, auditing, financial advising, tax collection, delinquent-tax collection, trash collection, and district communications. We identify [Schwartz, Page & Harding](https://www.sphllp.com/) as counsel; [Municipal District Services](https://mdswater.com/) as operator; [Pape-Dawson](https://www.pape-dawson.com/) as engineer; [Municipal Accounts & Consulting](https://municipalaccounts.com/) as bookkeeper; [Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.us/) as auditor; [Masterson Advisors](https://mastersonadvisors.com/) as financial advisor; [Wheeler & Associates](https://www.wheelerassoc.com/) as Tax Collector; and other contractors serving delinquent-tax, trash, and media functions.

There is nothing inherently unlawful about a governmental district hiring private consultants. Special districts routinely rely on outside professionals. The problem arises when outsourcing becomes a substitute for oversight. An elected or appointed board cannot outsource its responsibility to govern. Consultants may advise. They may prepare documents. They may operate facilities. They may provide professional expertise. But the ultimate responsibility for the district remains with its governing board.

> 
That principle becomes especially important when the same small circle of consultants has served multiple related districts for many years; going back to 1986.
- The question for Grand Lakes is therefore not simply: "How much are we paying our consultants?" 
- It is: **What controls exist to ensure that every consultant is acting solely in the interests of the district and its residents?**
- And, perhaps more importantly: **Who independently checks the people doing the checking?**
