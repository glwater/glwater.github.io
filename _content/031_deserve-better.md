For decades, Grand Lakes residents have built homes, raised families, paid taxes, paid utility bills and invested their lives in this community. 

> 
Many families have been here for 20 years or more.
- They deserve water districts that are transparent, economical, professionally managed, independently supervised and accountable to the people.
- They deserve boards that govern.
- They deserve consultants who serve.
- They deserve records they can trust.
- They deserve financial information they can understand.
- They deserve the opportunity to make informed decisions about rates, taxes and debt.
- And when credible evidence of wrongdoing exists, they deserve an impartial investigation.
- The goal should not be revenge.
- The goal should not be political victory.
- The goal should not be replacing one group of insiders with another.
- The goal should be restoring lawful, transparent, and accountable government to Grand Lakes.

>
That means asking serious and difficult questions.
- It means following the money.
- It means preserving the records.
- It means examining the evidence.
- It means allowing independent authorities to determine what actually happened.
- And if violations occurred, it means holding the responsible parties accountable and recovering whatever public resources can lawfully be recovered.

> 
The Grand Lakes community is not asking for anything extraordinary; we are asking for something fundamental:
- **Government must answer to the people.**
- And when public money, public records, public infrastructure, and public trust are involved, **no board, attorney, consultant, contractor, or other private interest is above the law.**
- Grand Lakes deserves transparency.
- Grand Lakes deserves accountability.
- **Grand Lakes deserves justice.**
